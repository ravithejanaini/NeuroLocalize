Drawing conventions

  • The cross-section is drawn three times wider than true, so tracts can be told apart.
  • Time is slowed 333×. Speed ratios between fibres are kept exactly: a C-fibre pulse really is fifteen times slower than an Aδ pulse.
  • Peripheral and corticospinal speeds are sourced. Speeds of the posterior-column and spinothalamic axons inside the cord are not, and are drawn at one illustrative rate.
  • The segment-to-vertebra ruler is interpolated between sourced anchors, with every vertebra drawn the same height.
  • The arm is drawn shortened and the plexus is schematic. What is sourced is the order of things: trunks between the scalenes and over the first rib, divisions behind the clavicle, cords named around the axillary artery, and where each branch leaves its nerve.
Every finding is computed from a sourced knowledge base and checked against expectations written before the engine existed. Chips link to the source behind each group; “contested” and “convention” mark what is not settled. Source and tests: github.com/ravithejanaini/NeuroLocalize.
