# P41 — Finishing what P37 to P40 left half-done

Written on 2026-10-06. No new anatomy: three things the last four phases named as limits.

## 1. Every drawn part can be named, not only those above the cord

P39 named the marks of the brain and the visual pathway. This phase adds four kinds:

- **A part of the cord's cross-section** at the slice — the ten the model reasons with, each
  side. The card quotes the rows the engine reads for that part: the pathway it lies on, and the
  autonomic rows that test it.
- **A spinal root**, dorsal and ventral together, at every segment. The card quotes what a root
  carries, the reflexes served by that segment, and at T1 the sympathetic outflow.
- **A place beyond the roots** — a trunk, a cord of the plexus, a nerve at a named site. The card
  quotes the row of its trunk, cord, part of the plexus or nerve, and names the lung apex where
  that takes it.
- **An end organ of the cranial nerves.** An organ has no lesion of its own, so its card says
  which nerves reach it instead of what is lost, and quotes only the rows those courses were
  built from.

The rule of P39 holds: a card states nothing of its own. Tests hold each kind: every part has a
name unlike any other and at least one sourced statement; a row that names a part of the cord in
its own data is quoted for that part; each part of the cord does what its rows say; an organ's
statements are exactly the rows of the courses that reach it.

Looking straight down the cord, everything along the limbs falls on top of the slice, so there a
tap takes only the slice. A part of the cord is drawn as a wide disc, so a tap anywhere on it
counts, and the nearest part in proportion to its size wins.

## 2. The findings read as sentences

A finding was the examination's label, a dash and a value: "this eye does not adduct — present".
Now a sign that is simply there is named without "present"; a cranial sign names its side once
("On the left: the eye does not adduct"); and a group reads "Right side: 25 muscles weak".

## 3. The phone

Ten station buttons wrapped into two rows that covered a third of a phone's stage. They are now
one row that scrolls sideways and keeps the chosen station in sight. The list of parts sits under
it instead of across the middle of the stage. The Vision and Sympathetic stations stand further
back on a stage narrower than it is tall, so the whole pathway fits.

## Limits

- The parts of the cord are named only at the slice, and only once the camera is close.
- The list of parts by name is long — about three hundred and forty entries in twelve groups.
- On a phone the card covers the lower half of the stage while it is open.
- The lateral horn at most segments reports nothing: the model reads it only at the ciliospinal
  and micturition centres.
- No mutation run: nothing the engine reads changed.
